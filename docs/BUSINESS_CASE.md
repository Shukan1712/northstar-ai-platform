# Northstar Business Case

## Problem

Commercial decisions often require employees to assemble context from customer records, orders, invoices, inventory, service cases, contracts, and policy documents. The work is slow, inconsistent, and difficult to review when evidence is spread across systems.

## Proposed product

Northstar Insight gives employees a permission-aware decision workspace. It produces customer briefings, highlights delivery and payment risk, retrieves current policies, and shows the evidence used for every answer.

## Users and value

| User | Decision supported | Demonstrated value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account manager | Prepare for a customer meeting | Combines commercial, service, finance, and contract context |
| Finance analyst | Prioritize overdue exposure | Uses verified balances and aging status |
| Operations manager | Investigate delivery and inventory exceptions | Connects orders, stock, and service issues |
| Executive | Review business health | Presents deterministic KPIs and priority exceptions |
| AI administrator | Govern platform behavior | Reviews audit events, evaluation cases, and configuration status |

## Why this is an AI project

AI is used where language and reasoning create value: interpreting a business question, choosing approved tools, combining heterogeneous evidence, explaining implications, and presenting a concise recommendation. Deterministic code remains responsible for authorization, totals, status logic, and risk calculations.

## Success measures

- Time required to prepare an account briefing
- Percentage of material claims supported by a citation
- Accuracy of invoice, delivery, and inventory calculations
- Unauthorized-data access rate, with a target of zero
- Safe-refusal rate for unsupported or action-taking requests
- Employee usefulness rating for recommended next questions

## Portfolio narrative

This project demonstrates product strategy, enterprise architecture, data modeling, full-stack engineering, AI orchestration, retrieval, access control, auditability, evaluation, and responsible-AI design in one coherent business scenario.
